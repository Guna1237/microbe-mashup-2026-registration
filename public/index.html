Registration Guidelines

Complete Basic Registration first. Use the Registration ID sent to your email to book Events & Workshops.

1. Basic Registration

  • Basic registration is mandatory for everyone and costs ₹800 per person for individual registration.
  • Fill out the Basic Registration form with your personal and contact details.
  • After payment is confirmed, each participant receives a unique Registration ID by email.

2. Group Registration

  • Register individually or as a group of 3, 4 or 5. One group leader completes registration for everyone.
  • The leader enters the name, phone number and email of each member, including their own details.
  • Groups of 3–4 pay ₹750 per person. Groups of 5 pay ₹700 per person.
  • Each person receives their own Registration ID by email and should keep it for further registrations.

3. Events & Workshops Registration

  • Use the separate Events & Workshops form after Basic Registration is confirmed.
  • Enter your own Registration ID from your confirmation email to proceed. This also applies to every group member.

4. Important Instructions

  • Check that all personal and contact details are accurate, especially email addresses.
  • Use your own Registration ID for later bookings and check your registered email for updates.
  • For registration queries, contact the organizing committee.
